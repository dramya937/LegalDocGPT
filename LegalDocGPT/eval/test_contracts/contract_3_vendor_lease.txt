EQUIPMENT LEASE AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

This Equipment Lease and Maintenance Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into
between Ironclad Fleet Leasing LLC ("Lessor") and Harbor Logistics Corp.
("Lessee").

1. LEASED EQUIPMENT
Lessor shall lease to Lessee the equipment described in Exhibit A (the
"Equipment") for use in Lessee's ordinary course of business.

2. LEASE TERM AND PAYMENT
The lease term is thirty-six (36) months. Lessee shall pay monthly lease
payments as set forth in Exhibit A, due on the first day of each month. Late
payments more than ten (10) days overdue shall incur a late fee of 5% of the
overdue amount.

3. TERMINATION
Lessee may not terminate this Agreement prior to the end of the lease term
except upon Lessor's uncured material breach. Lessor may terminate immediately
and repossess the Equipment without notice if Lessee misses two (2) or more
consecutive monthly payments.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
LESSOR'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT, OR
OTHERWISE, SHALL NOT EXCEED ONE HUNDRED DOLLARS ($100), REGARDLESS OF THE
NUMBER OF CLAIMS OR THE FORM OF ACTION. LESSEE WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO SEEK
CONSEQUENTIAL, INCIDENTAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES OF ANY KIND.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
Lessee shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Lessor from any and all
claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including attorneys' fees) arising from
Lessee's use, operation, or possession of the Equipment, regardless of whether
such claims arise from Lessor's negligence.

6. MAINTENANCE
Lessor shall perform routine maintenance on the Equipment on a quarterly basis.
Lessee is responsible for reporting any malfunction within twenty-four (24)
hours of discovery.

7. INSURANCE
Lessee shall maintain comprehensive general liability insurance covering the
Equipment in an amount no less than $1,000,000 per occurrence, naming Lessor as
an additional insured.

8. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month
periods unless Lessee provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety
(90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any dispute
shall be resolved exclusively in the state or federal courts located in Harris
County, Texas.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the date
first written above.
